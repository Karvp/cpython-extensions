# Code of Conduct

`cpython-extensions` expects technical collaboration to remain respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

When reviewing or discussing changes:

- critique implementations, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than people;
- distinguish measured facts from hypotheses and clearly label uncertainty;
- keep disagreement specific enough that another contributor can reproduce or evaluate it;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publication of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

Security-sensitive disagreements should not be litigated in public before triage. Use the private reporting path described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities or verifier/safety-boundary failures.
